ℹ️ About GramCare AI – Rural Decision-Support System
Version 1.1.0

1. Purpose & Mission

GramCare AI is an AI-powered rural healthcare assistant designed as a clinical decision-support tool for rural patients, ASHA workers, and digital kiosks (Jan Aushadhi Kendras & Common Service Centres). It tackles doctor shortages, long travel distances, and delayed disease detection by providing early symptom triage, longitudinal risk progression, and affordable generic medicine guidance.

2. Important Clarification on Medical Scope

"GramCare AI does not replace doctors. It acts as an early decision-support system to determine whether home care is sufficient, or if clinical consultation and emergency referral are necessary."

3. Longitudinal Risk Engine

Unlike isolated triage tools that only evaluate a single consultation, GramCare AI queries previous consultations within the last 30–60 days. If key symptoms recur or worsen across multiple visits, the system escalates the risk level and alerts the ASHA worker that the patient may have a progressing or chronic condition requiring clinical diagnosis.

4. Affordable Generic Medicines

To reduce the financial burden of healthcare on rural families, GramCare AI highlights generic molecule equivalents available under the Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Jan Aushadhi Pariyojana, offering estimated cost savings of 50% to 80% over commercial branded drugs.
